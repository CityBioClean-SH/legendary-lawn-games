Orange County FAQ

Common questions from our OC customers

We deliver to all of Orange County including Irvine, Newport Beach, Anaheim, Huntington Beach, Laguna Beach, Costa Mesa, Fullerton, Mission Viejo, Dana Point, San Clemente, and everywhere in between. If you're in the OC metro area, we've got you covered.

Yes! Delivery, setup, and pickup are completely free within our standard Orange County service area (roughly a 20-mile radius from central OC). For locations in south county near San Clemente or north toward La Habra, a small mileage fee may apply.

We recommend booking 2-4 weeks in advance, especially for weekends during peak summer season (May-October). Orange County summers are perfect for outdoor events, so our calendar fills up fast. That said, we often have same-week availability for weekday events.

We love beach setups! Our games work great on flat sandy areas at Huntington Beach, Newport Beach, and Dana Point. We recommend cornhole, bocce ball, and ladder toss for beach events. Giant Jenga works too on a flat surface. Just let us know your beach location when booking and we'll recommend the best games for sand.
