The Story Behind Legendary

How a love for backyard games became a business

Our Story

We started Legendary Lawn Games because we noticed something at every event we attended — the moment someone set up a cornhole board or a giant Jenga tower, people put down their phones, stopped standing awkwardly by the bar, and actually started having fun.

It always seemed like the best moments at any gathering happened around the games. Not the DJ booth, not the buffet table — the games. Kids, adults, grandparents, coworkers who barely knew each other — everyone gravitated toward them. And yet, finding quality giant games to rent was surprisingly hard. Most options were beat-up, overpriced, or came with a "you pick it up, you set it up" hassle.

What started as a few games in a garage has grown into a premium rental service across 5 metro areas. But our mission hasn't changed: make every gathering legendary.

We deliver. We set up. We pick up. You just play. That's it. No hauling equipment in your trunk, no spending your party time assembling games, no worrying about returns. We show up early, arrange everything, and disappear. When your event is over, we come back and take it all away.
