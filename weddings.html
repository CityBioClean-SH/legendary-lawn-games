Wedding FAQ

Everything you need to know before booking

We get it, weather happens. If rain is in the forecast, we'll work with you to move games under a covered area (pavilion, tent, porch, etc.). If the weather makes outdoor games truly impossible and there's no covered backup space, we'll reschedule your delivery or issue a full refund. We monitor the forecast starting 48 hours before your event and stay in close contact.

It depends on the package. For 3 games (Cocktail Hour), you'll want about a 20' x 30' area. For the full 10-game Ultimate Wedding package, a space around 40' x 60' is ideal. But don't stress about exact measurements. Send us a photo of your venue and we'll tell you exactly what fits and where to put it.

Yes! Custom monogram wraps on our cornhole boards are available for an additional $49. Send us your initials, wedding date, and color palette, and we'll create beautiful custom boards that match your theme. They also make for incredible photo backdrops. Just give us 2 weeks lead time.

Absolutely. Full white-glove service is included in every wedding package. We arrive 2 hours before your ceremony to get everything set up perfectly, and we return at the end of the night to quietly pack it all up. You, your wedding party, and your vendors don't have to touch a thing.

We recommend booking 2-4 weeks before your wedding date, but popular weekends (especially in spring and fall) book up fast. If you have a Saturday in peak season, booking 6-8 weeks out guarantees you get exactly what you want. That said, we've pulled off last-minute bookings with as little as 48 hours notice if our calendar allows.
