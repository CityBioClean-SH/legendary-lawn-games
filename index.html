Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

We deliver all games to your event location, set everything up, and pick it all up when your event ends. Delivery and setup are included free within our standard service areas (20-mile radius from each metro center). Beyond that, a small mileage fee applies.

We recommend booking 2-4 weeks in advance, especially for weekends during peak season (April-October). However, we often have same-week and even next-day availability. Check our online calendar for real-time availability.

No stress! You can reschedule for free up to 24 hours before your event. If rain pops up unexpectedly day-of, we'll work with you to find the next available date at no additional cost. Many of our games also work great under covered patios and pavilions.

Normal wear and tear is expected — don't worry about it. For significant damage (broken pieces, stains, etc.), we hold a $200 pre-authorization on your card at booking. This is NOT charged unless damage occurs. If it does, we only charge the actual repair/replacement cost.

It depends on how many games you book. As a rule of thumb: each game needs about a 10x10 ft area. A typical 5-game package needs roughly 30x20 ft of open space. We'll help you plan the layout when you book — just tell us about your venue and we'll make it work.

Yes! Custom cornhole boards are one of our most popular add-ons, especially for corporate events and weddings. Add your company logo, wedding monogram, or any design for an additional $100-200. Just add it when you build your package — we need at least 2 weeks lead time for custom boards.
