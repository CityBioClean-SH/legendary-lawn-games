Birthday FAQ

Everything you need to know before booking

Our games work for ages 5 and up. Younger kids love Giant Tic-Tac-Toe, Ring Toss, and Yard Pong because the rules are simple and the pieces are big. Older kids, teens, and adults get competitive with Giant Jenga, Cornhole, and Connect Four. We've done parties for 6-year-olds and 60-year-olds — everyone has a blast.

For the Backyard Bash (3 games), about 20' x 20' works great. For the Party Pro (5 games), aim for 30' x 30'. The Epic Birthday (8 games) is best with 40' x 40' or more. Don't stress if you're not sure — send us a photo of your space and we'll tell you exactly what fits.

Absolutely! We deliver to parks, pavilions, community centers, restaurants with outdoor space, and any other party venue. Just double-check that your venue allows outside equipment (most do), and we'll handle delivery and setup. Some public parks require a permit for events — we can advise on that when you book.

Weather happens! If rain is in the forecast, we'll work with you to move games under a covered area (garage, patio, pavilion, tent). If the weather makes it truly impossible and there's no backup space, we'll reschedule for free or issue a full refund. We start monitoring the forecast 48 hours out and stay in touch.

Standard rentals are for the duration of your party, typically 3-5 hours. We deliver and set up before guests arrive and pick up after the party ends. Need the games for longer — say an all-day event or overnight? No problem, just let us know and we'll work out the details.
